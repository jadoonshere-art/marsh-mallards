# Marsh Mallards

A browser duck hunting game that runs on phones and desktops. It is a single file with no downloads and no build step. All art and sound are drawn and synthesized in code.

## How to play

- Click or tap to fire. Pellets take time to arrive, so aim ahead of the duck. Fast and far ducks need more lead.
- The ring around your crosshair is your spread. It gets wider with distance.
- R reloads. 1, 2 and 3 switch guns. P pauses. M mutes.
- Let three ducks escape and the game is over.

## Guns

| Gun | Shells | Style |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pump Classic | 4 | Balanced, pump action |
| Double Barrel | 2 | Wide pattern, fast follow-up |
| Semi-Auto | 6 | Quick shots, tighter pattern |

## Birds

Mallard, teal, wood duck, pintail, goose (two hits) and a rare gold duck that gives an extra life. The white egret is protected, so shooting it costs a life.

A light hit only wounds a duck. It staggers and loses height, and you can finish it with a second shot.

## Bonus stage

Every third level you drift out on a boat. A few ducks sit on the water. Fire once to flush them, then clear them all before they escape. A perfect clear gives a big bonus and an extra life. Anything less pays a smaller bonus per duck.

## Look and feel

Wings have separate tip feathers and a motion blur when flapping. Feathers and down drift and flutter after a hit, a dead shot triggers a short slow motion, and the screen kicks slightly on every shot. Static scenery is painted once and reused, so it runs smoothly on phones.

## Levels

Levels rotate through three flight styles:

1. Side cross: ducks fly across the marsh.
2. Straight on: ducks fly right at you.
3. Overhead: ducks pass over your head.

The first three levels use one style each. From level 4 on, most ducks follow the level's style and a few mix in from the others.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in any modern browser.

To put it online for free, turn on GitHub Pages for this repository and serve the main branch from the root folder.

## Notes

- Switches to lite graphics on its own if the frame rate is low.
- Gun designs are inspired by classic shotguns but use generic names.
